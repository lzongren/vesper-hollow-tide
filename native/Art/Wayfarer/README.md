# Magnolia Wayfarer

The fitted player uses a head and neck excerpt of the MakeHuman standard base mesh. The source OBJ explicitly releases the geometry under CC0. The original authors are Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. The fitting retains the facial topology, projects a new diffuse texture and assigns hair and skin to separate surfaces. [MakeHuman source mesh and license notice](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj)

The riding coat uses the original magnolia embroidery in `magnolia-silk.jpg`. The plum fitting projects [plum-face.jpg](plum-face.jpg), a makeup pass over the original portrait in [ruyi-face.jpg](ruyi-face.jpg), onto the eyelids, lips and facial surface. The older `magnolia-face.jpg` is preserved with the earlier fitting. The fitter narrows the jaw while preserving the original topology. The hair ornaments, coiled hair, pearl drops and layered garment are geometry attached to the combat skeleton. The fitted riding trousers and boots are skinned around the hip, knee, ankle and ball joints. The split coat uses a separate trouser yoke underneath the hip panels. The gloves, sword and scabbard carry over from the existing rig. [Full-length fittings and combat captures](../../../docs/runtime/lingbo-haitang/)

The two plum brocade streamers share a jade waist fastening. Their warp coordinate runs from the fastening to the forked tip. Regenerate the river-and-cloud embroidery with `python art/river_sash.py` and Pillow. [In-game movement](../../../docs/runtime/river-sash/)

The present fitting adds a four-lobed cloud collar over the riding coat. Each lobe maps the full embroidered magnolia to a separately weighted panel. Two lines of brass piping enclose the rose silk binding. The swept locks travel from an offset part toward the temples. The eyes use a smoother material than the surrounding face.

The cloud collar uses the built-in ImageGen textile source in [ruyi-embroidery.jpg](ruyi-embroidery.jpg). Its generation prompt follows.

## Plum blossom and butterfly fitting

The face carries a five-petal cinnabar *huadian*, a carmine lip glaze and a soft cheek wash. The hairline sits on cross sections of the fitted head. Two alpha-masked panels break up its edge at the temples. The blue-green butterfly wings have gilt outlines, wire veins, small antennae and a pearl body. The open and closed lids use one skinned mesh with a bilateral blink morph. Regenerate the makeup and hair mask with Pillow using `python art/plum_fitting.py`. ## White-jade magnolia fitting

In the playable Yulan fitting, seventeen overlapping back locks transition from the head to the neck and upper spine. Six shorter locks frame the cheeks. Their four original opacity textures share a 1024-pixel atlas. Three carved magnolias of different sizes sit on a curved gilt twig. Two beaded chains fall beside the flowers. The pleated celadon skirt panels share the thigh weights of the split riding skirt.

Run `python art/jade_hair.py` with Pillow and NumPy to reproduce the strand atlas and the facial texture. The face uses the plum portrait above, with reduced baked shadow under the eyes and nose. Each eyeball has its own chestnut iris and limbal ring sampled from the portrait. The upper lash line follows the fitted eyelid, and the face material supports the blink morph. ## Haitang swordswoman

The playable fitting registers the CC0 adult [MakeHuman target](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/targets/macrodetails/asian-female-young.target) to the combat rig at the eye sockets and tapers the change out at the neck seam. To regenerate the offsets, run `python art/fit_camellia_head.py base.obj asian-female-young.target` with the upstream OBJ linked above. The UV projection maps the new closed-lid complexion onto moving eyelids. Separate iris geometry carries the chestnut pigment generated with `python art/camellia_iris.py` (Pillow and NumPy). Each upper lash follows the authored rim through the same blink as the skin. Run `python art/camellia_hairline.py` (Pillow) to regenerate the feathered hairline. The hair cards, narrow cloud collar and gold-wire ear pendants are original skinned geometry. The costume is a fictional late-Ming-inspired design.

Built-in ImageGen sources used by the game are [the skin albedo](camellia-face.jpg) and [the embroidered silk albedo](camellia-silk.jpg). Generation prompts follow.

Fine tonal jacquard diaper weave behind, extremely fine satin stitches individually visible, real textile macro photography softly lit. Whole motif within center 72 percent, all edges plain matching mauve ground. Luxurious restrained palette and negative space, no borders, folds, objects, lettering, badges, watermarks or cast shadows. This is a texture for a small formal cloud collar on a playable classical Chinese swordswoman.

> An original, very beautiful adult Chinese woman swordswoman aged 27, styled like an understated late Ming period costume drama. A perfectly frontal, straight-on orthographic facial albedo photograph for a real-time 3D digital double. Square frame from the top of her swept-back hair to just below her chin. Face centered and occupies 70% of width, both ears visible, both eyes GENTLY CLOSED, no eyelashes sticking outward, expression serene, corner of closed mouth slightly lifted. Distinctive softly arched fine willow-leaf brows, delicate oval cheeks, smooth natural ivory-olive complexion with subtle rosy cheek color, elegant nose, defined cupid's bow and soft muted rose vermilion lips. Bright extremely even cross-polarized flat light, natural peach fuzz and tiny freckles close up, no strong directional shadow or specular highlights. Black hair pulled back cleanly away from her forehead, temples and ears, plain neutral gray background, bare neck, no jewelry, ornaments, forehead marks, lettering or accessories. Aim at eye seams near 45% of image height, nose tip 62%, lip seam 75%, chin 91%. It must look like a genuinely photographed human woman, never anime, doll or plastic, minimal retouching.

> Use this exact last closed-eyed original adult Chinese swordswoman portrait as a face albedo for the playable character. Preserve the exact centered framing, eyelids, skin tone, pale rose lips, brows, cheeks, ears and facial anatomy. Soften only the hair shadow by the temples and keep all light evenly diffuse. Square close front portrait. No lettering, ornament or clothing.

The skin material combines the original complexion with a registered 2048² tangent normal and linear roughness map. Rebuild the two maps with `python art/camellia_complexion.py` from the repository root, with NumPy and Pillow installed. Pore relief varies across the nose, cheeks and eyelids. The vermilion mask follows the lip colour in the source atlas. Both maps use the portrait coverage painted into the second UV channel.

The eyes use the fitted globe dimensions to reproject the iris beneath the corneal highlight. The game samples visible opponents, follows the animated head pose through combat and releases the gaze when the target leaves. The current outfit adds an original gilt-wire swallow pin with layered enamel feathers, seed pearls and a forked tail. The two-sided ornament is fitted to the head joint in `jade_swallow.py`. The cloud collar reserves the embroidered flowers while overdyeing the ground lake blue. Additional swept sections follow the fitted forehead, while the cheeks, lips, brows and lids use the existing head topology and open-eye rig. [Unreal runtime views](../../../docs/runtime/jade-swallow/)

The begonia fitting lifts the resting mouth corners, softens the cheeks, and seats the swept temples closer to the brow. Two silk hair ribbons fold over a small gilt flower and descend along the skinned upper back. ## Paired facial expressions

The original closed-eye [spectre portrait](spectre-face.jpg) establishes the identity. An ImageGen edit of that exact source supplied the [open eyelid guide](portrait-open.jpg). Only the skin around the eyes samples the second texture during play. The rotating brown irises and moving upper lashes have their own geometry. The flat guide and the engine renders are identified individually in the [comparison](../../../docs/runtime/portrait-match/).

> Edit only the two closed eyelids of this square frontal photographic reference: the very same adult Chinese woman now opens her eyes in a completely relaxed neutral gaze toward the lens. Naturally almond shaped, subtly hooded upper lids covering top quarters of rich VERY dark warm brown irises, small dark pupils, gentle narrow openings, relaxed lower lids, single broad soft window reflection in each eye. Keep her identical identity, frontal square framing, facial proportions, makeup, landmarks, nose, cheeks, eyebrows, lip shape, hair, colour, matte diffuse lighting and background. Maintain exact eyelid corners and the existing restrained cinnabar line at their outer tips. Absolutely no change outside the local eye area. No other expression. Production matched open-eye reference photo for a rigged 3D face.

The bake uses Google's [Face Landmarker](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker?tab=Python) with NumPy, SciPy and Pillow. Run `python art/fit_portrait_reference.py face_landmarker.task path/to/ShuanghuaHaitang.glb` against the baseline mesh, followed by `python art/check_portrait_match.py face_landmarker.task` after recording the native captures. The launcher consumes the baked deformation fields and eyelid curves directly. Both reference images were produced for this project.

The comparison used MediaPipe 1.2.3 and the Face Landmarker task bundle with SHA-256 `64184e229b263107bc2b804c6625db1341ff2bb731874b0bcc2fe6544e0bc9ff`. The baseline GLB is generated by the native launcher at commit `dcc3ab248be2f9faad208718194dbe93bdf478b9`. The intermediate calibration renders are included with the comparison.

## Digital human build

The current playable head uses Epic's Ada sample DNA as its three-dimensional base. The local bake evaluates the LOD0 neutral mesh, skin weights, neutral joint orientations, control-to-joint matrices and corresponding corrective blendshapes. Seven posed results are retargeted to the game rig. The facial sculpt uses the open frontal identity guide and a newly drawn profile. The skin uses an unwrapped diffuse, registered normal and roughness, a subsurface profile and independently moving eyes, lashes, teeth and inner eye tissue. [Native captures](../../../docs/runtime/digital-human/)

The Windows launcher retrieves the [official example and DNA reader](https://github.com/EpicGames/MetaHuman-DNA-Calibration/tree/8297ff1bd6f1692a6e221aa8395a66486e5a53c9) and verifies pinned SHA-256 hashes. The SDK, DNA, source maps, converted face and interpreter are kept locally in the project's `Saved/DigitalHuman`. The public repository contains the original code, character calibration, original concept images and rendered evidence. Epic's inputs have their [own license](https://github.com/EpicGames/MetaHuman-DNA-Calibration/blob/8297ff1bd6f1692a6e221aa8395a66486e5a53c9/LICENSE).

Built-in ImageGen produced [the profile guide](sculpt-profile.jpg) by editing the existing open eye portrait. It supplies an art-directed silhouette. It is not a photographic scan. The generation prompt was:

> Production sculpt reference for this identical adult Chinese woman. Rotate her head exactly ninety degrees to a true right-facing strict lateral profile, orthographic long lens, nose points to the right edge. Keep the same identity, calm eyes naturally open, relaxed closed mouth, forehead flower, hair pinned away from cheek and ear, dark centre parted hair, plain neutral grey background, even diffuse illumination, true anatomical ear, no jewelry. Preserve the same crop, eye level, nose and lip spacing, jaw height and delicacy. One single portrait, no lettering. This will be used to read the silhouette of the nose, lip and chin of a playable game sculpt.

To reproduce the optional art registration, install NumPy, SciPy, Pillow and MediaPipe, then run `python art/register_digital_sculpt.py face_landmarker.task` with the bundle previously identified above. The result is read by the native bake, which uses only its private embedded interpreter and the checked reference images.
