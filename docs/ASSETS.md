# Asset provenance

Story names, encounter rules, architecture, effects, cloak/hat/bell details and synthesized audio were created for this project. The realism revision also uses third-party CC0 models and scanned PBR materials, listed in [the machine-readable asset manifest](imported-assets.json). No assets from Bloodborne or Wuchang are included.

- `public/art/vesper-cover.png`: generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for this project on 2026-11-11. The in-game scene uses separate 3D geometry and textures; the cover is concept/title art, not a gameplay screenshot.
- `public/favicon.svg`: original geometric project emblem.
- `src/scene.ts`: original temple geometry, costume additions, cloth deformation, skeletal posing, lighting and effects. Imported actors retain their original mesh and texture provenance.
- `src/audio.ts`: original synthesis and composition code; no recordings or samples from other games.
- `public/fonts/cormorant-garamond.ttf`: Cormorant Garamond from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License. License included beside the file.
- `public/fonts/inter.ttf`: Inter from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License. License included beside the file.
- Three.js, TypeScript, Vite, and other dependencies retain their upstream licenses; see the lockfile for exact versions.

## Imported CC0 art

The six GLB files in `public/models/` are from VibeAssets: Gothic plate-armour knight, winged wraith, horned sorcerer, longsword, fir trees and dead pine. The source catalogue identifies several as Meshy-generated. These are prototype art assets, not commissioned historical Chinese characters. Costume additions and material treatment are authored in `src/scene.ts`.

Poly Haven supplies cobblestone_floor_001 and castle_brick_02_red (2K diffuse, normal and roughness maps), rock_boulder_cracked, wood_planks_grey and rusty_metal_02 (1K maps), and the 1K dikhololo_night HDR environment. All are CC0; exact source links and bundled filenames are in `imported-assets.json`. Assets are served from this repository, never hotlinked at runtime.

The game's MIT license applies to our code; fonts keep their OFL licenses and imported art keeps its CC0 dedication.

## Cover-art generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original dark fantasy action game title-screen artwork, cinematic wide 16:9 landscape. No words, letters, typography, logos or watermark. An abandoned tidal monastery of black stone rising from luminous mist at night. Right of center, a lone masked swordswoman with long ragged vermilion cloak and an elegant narrow sword, seen from behind at medium distance, faces an enormous ancient bronze ritual bell suspended within a broken cathedral arch. A delicate constellation halo and a pale eclipsed moon above. Pale feathers drifting through air, shallow reflective water, wet obsidian steps, a few warm votive candles, cypress silhouettes. East Asian ceremonial shapes blended with austere gothic ruins, completely original world and costume, not any existing game character. Painterly prestige concept art, exquisite intricate material detail, atmospheric depth, restrained ivory / charcoal / oxidized teal / muted cinnabar palette. Strong silhouette and beautiful composition. Left 45 percent relatively quiet dark fog for UI title and text overlay, no big objects there. Rich light and contrast around central-right figure and bell. Elegant melancholy, haunting beauty, no gore.

The original Tide Bell used by the Bellwarden and the temple is generated by [cast_bell.py](../art/cast_bell.py). Its 44,800 triangles include the scalloped shell, inner vault, clapper and suspension loop. The GLB embeds 1024-pixel bronze color, occlusion/roughness/metallic and normal maps. Run the script with Python, NumPy and Pillow to rebuild it.

## Engraved courts

Each boss has an original 12.7 m engraved stone disc. The Bellwarden's lotus, the Heron's feather vanes and the Saint's flame tongues use different curved inlays. The surrounding apron has individually bevelled slabs with staggered joints. The browser loads the chosen court's 1536-pixel base color, tangent normal and packed occlusion/roughness/metallic maps when entering the encounter. Run `python art/court_seals.py` with NumPy and Pillow to bake the maps from the authored curves and the bundled Poly Haven rock scan.

[The Bellwarden](screenshots/court-lotus.jpg) · [The Veiled Heron](screenshots/court-plume.jpg) · [Saint of Cinders](screenshots/court-cinder.jpg). These images were captured in the browser production build during combat at 1440×900.

The Heron moon gate, glazed coping, bamboo branches, Cinder arch and oil-lamp niches are original geometry in `src/landmarks.ts`. The cliffs and suspended bronze lamp use [Rock Face 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_01) and [Chinese Chandelier](https://polyhaven.com/a/chinese_chandelier) by Poly Haven, licensed CC0. `art/landmark_scans.py` packs their pinned geometry and 1536-pixel maps for the browser after the native source downloader verifies the original files.

The pilgrim's mantle carries a paired heron crest, woven ground, feather stitches and cloud-scroll hem. Rebuild its 1024-pixel color and tangent normal maps using `art/pilgrim_brocade.py` with NumPy and Pillow. The curved mantle is attached to the upper spine. `src/pilgrim.ts` poses the right arm and hand, and samples the equipped blade for its trail. [RTX 5080 gameplay](runtime/pilgrim/README.md).

The Veiled Heron now retains the CC0 wraith skeleton and uses original visible geometry for her face, coiffure, clothing, paired sabres and wings. The wing fans use the existing quill image. The court panels and embroidered costume share the Heron silk image. The mask and its fittings are also included as a five-part binary glTF in the native art directory.

## Crane paintings for the moon gate

The built-in ImageGen tool generated `public/art/crane-fresco.jpg` for the playable garden. Each side of the porch uses half of the atlas. The lamp, cast shadows, projecting stone sills and dark wood mouldings are rendered in 3D. The asset uses the following generation prompt.

> Game environment albedo texture, orthographic conservation photograph flat on: one wide landscape late-Ming-inspired Chinese mineral-pigment temple mural. Two complementary halves separated only by empty plaster at exact center, no physical seam. On LEFT half a single large elegant ivory crane landing with one raised sweeping wing under windswept pine branches, on RIGHT half a second large white crane folding one wing among peonies, both looking inward. Original handmade gongbi lines, warm ochre and worn cinnabar plaster, deep malachite pine, dusty azurite cloud ribbons, traces of aged gilt on feather tips. Strong bird silhouettes at thumbnail scale. White crane body and red crown must be easily distinguishable against muted blue-green ground. Offwhite spalls and small patches of exposed clay at the outer edges, mineral pigment gritty and delicately cracked, asymmetrically weathered. Paint fills every edge, no border, frame, window, photography equipment, text, labels, inscriptions, shadows, perspective, gradient lighting or UI. Uniform diffuse illumination allows physical relighting in-game. Wide 3:2 horizontal atlas; two equal half-images.
