# The Bellwarden in motion

[Combat recording with game audio](bellwarden.mp4) · [Raised bell at 1440p](raised-bell.jpg)

The recording uses normal movement, attack, parry and dodge inputs in the Drowned Belfry. Edge ran the game on a Windows RTX 5080. The 1080p video uses timestamped browser frames and audio from the game's master output captured during the same encounter. The music, footsteps and impacts play at their original speed.

The Warden has separate movements for a sweep, a ground strike and a charge. The driving hand carries the shaft while the other hand reaches for a lower grip. The wrist pose is continuous across the change from anticipation to damage to recovery. The player’s mantle lags sideways motion at the hem while its collar stays attached. The ground warning follows the simulated range and angle.

A separate six-second sequence ran in each fight at 2560 × 1440. The recorded browser frame interval was 16.7 ms at the median in all three. The Bellwarden reached 50.3 ms at the 95th percentile, while the other two reached 16.8 ms. [Raw intervals and renderer identification](frame-intervals.json)
