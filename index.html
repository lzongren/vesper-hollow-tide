Gold tells can be parried. Red tells must be dodged or avoided. Tells lock direction before impact.

Fill the enemy’s posture bar with strikes and parries. When they kneel, land a heavy strike for a devastating riposte.

Strike back soon after being hit to reclaim the pale portion of your health. Every boss changes at half health.
